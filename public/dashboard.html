Felicia Bakes on your phone

Open your bakery desk from a home-screen icon. Each person signs in with their own dashboard details.

iPhone: Open this page in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen and Add.

Android: Open this page in Chrome, then use Install app or Add to Home screen in the browser menu.

An internet connection is required. Orders, customers and email are not available offline.
